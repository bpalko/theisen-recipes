## Ingredients

- 6 apples, peeled and sliced
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
- 1 cup old-fashioned oats
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 8 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Toss apples with lemon, sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Spread in a 9x13 dish.
2. Mix oats, flour, brown sugar, and salt. Cut in butter until clumpy. Scatter over the apples.
3. Bake 40 to 45 minutes until bubbling and the topping is brown. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
