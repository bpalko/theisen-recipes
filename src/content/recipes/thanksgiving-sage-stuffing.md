## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs country bread, cubed and dried
- 8 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 onions, diced
- 4 celery stalks, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh sage
- 1 tbsp chopped thyme
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- 2 1/2 cups chicken broth, plus more as needed
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Black pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Butter a 9x13 dish. Put the bread in a large bowl.
2. Cook onion and celery in butter until soft, 12 minutes. Add garlic, sage, and thyme.
3. Pour over the bread with parsley. Whisk broth, eggs, salt, and pepper; toss until moist but not soggy.
4. Pack into the dish. Cover and bake 25 minutes, then uncover and bake 20 to 25 minutes until the top is crisp.
