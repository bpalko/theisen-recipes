## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a rimmed half sheet with parchment.
2. Whisk butter and sugars, then eggs and vanilla.
3. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold in chips.
4. Press into the pan. Bake 18 to 22 minutes until the edges are set and the center is just done.
5. Cool 10 minutes, then cut into squares.
