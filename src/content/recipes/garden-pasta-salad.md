## Ingredients

- 1 lb short pasta (rotini or shells)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup sliced olives
- 8 oz mozzarella pearls
- 1/2 cup chopped parsley
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Kosher salt and black pepper

## Steps

1. Cook pasta in salted water until just tender. Drain and rinse under cool water.
2. Whisk oil, vinegar, Dijon, seasoning, salt, and pepper.
3. Toss pasta with vegetables, olives, mozzarella, parsley, and dressing.
4. Chill at least 1 hour. Taste and add salt or vinegar before serving.
