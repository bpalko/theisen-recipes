## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 lbs beef chuck, cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 carrots, chunked
- 3 celery stalks, chunked
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup red wine or extra broth
- 4 cups beef broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 1/2 lbs Yukon gold potatoes, chunked
- 1 cup frozen peas

## Steps

1. Season the beef. Brown in batches in oil in a heavy pot. Set aside.
2. Cook onion, carrots, and celery 8 minutes. Stir in tomato paste and garlic.
3. Deglaze with wine. Return beef, add broth, bay, and thyme. Simmer covered 1 1/2 hours.
4. Add potatoes and cook until tender, 30 to 40 minutes. Stir in peas for 5 minutes. Season to taste.
