## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs Italian sausage links
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Fresh basil or parsley

## Steps

1. Brown sausages in oil in a large skillet, turning, about 8 minutes. Move to a plate.
2. Add peppers and onion. Cook until blistered and soft, 10 minutes. Stir in garlic and flakes.
3. Add tomatoes and salt. Nestle sausages back in and simmer 8 minutes until cooked through.
4. Scatter herbs and serve with bread or over polenta.
