## Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x5 loaf pan and line with parchment.
2. Stir bananas, melted butter, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla until smooth.
3. Fold in flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon just until no dry streaks remain. Add walnuts if using.
4. Scrape into the pan and bake 50 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with a few crumbs.
5. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack.
