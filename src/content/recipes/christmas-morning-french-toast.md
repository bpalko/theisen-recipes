## Ingredients

- 1 loaf brioche or challah, cubed
- 8 large eggs
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup maple syrup, plus more for serving
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 4 tbsp butter, cubed
- Powdered sugar

## Steps

1. Butter a 9x13 dish. Scatter the bread in an even layer.
2. Whisk eggs, milk, cream, maple, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Pour over the bread. Press down so it soaks.
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
4. Heat the oven to 350°F. Dot with butter. Bake 40 to 45 minutes until puffed and set.
5. Dust with powdered sugar and pass warm maple syrup.
