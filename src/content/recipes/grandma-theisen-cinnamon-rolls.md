## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole milk, warmed
- 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened (filling)
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tbsp cinnamon
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened (frosting)
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Steps

1. Stir the warm milk, yeast, and a pinch of sugar. Let it foam, about 5 minutes.
2. Beat in the remaining sugar, eggs, and melted butter. Add flour and salt until a soft dough forms.
3. Knead until smooth, 6 to 8 minutes. Rise in a greased bowl until doubled, about 1 hour.
4. Roll into a 12x18-inch rectangle. Spread with softened butter and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon.
5. Roll from the long side, cut into 12 pieces, and nestle in a buttered 9x13 pan. Rise 30 minutes.
6. Bake at 350°F until golden, 22 to 25 minutes. Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla; spread while warm.
