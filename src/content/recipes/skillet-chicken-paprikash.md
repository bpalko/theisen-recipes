## Ingredients

- 2 lbs bone-in chicken thighs
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp sweet paprika
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- Egg noodles, for serving

## Steps

1. Season the chicken well. Brown in oil in a wide skillet, 4 minutes per side. Set aside.
2. Cook onion and pepper in the drippings until soft, 8 minutes. Stir in garlic and paprika for 30 seconds.
3. Pour in broth, scrape the pan, and nestle the chicken back in. Cover and simmer 25 minutes.
4. Stir sour cream with a ladle of hot sauce, then fold it into the pan off the heat. Finish with parsley.
5. Serve over buttered egg noodles.
